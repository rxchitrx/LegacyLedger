## Question

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and notified rules), what is required or allowed on:

- (a) retention periods / storage limitation for personal data,
- (b) data-principal rights to correction and erasure, and how a Data Fiduciary must respond,
- (c) exemptions — especially retention required for legal or regulatory obligations and for legal claims (succession / probate disputes)?

Deliverable: a concise, cited fact sheet feeding ticket 13 (DPDP — retention and erasure vs permanent records).

**Findings:** [`../research/dpdp-obligations.md`](../research/dpdp-obligations.md)

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-10-18 by a research subagent during charting. Key facts: the substantive duties (including erasure under s.8(7) and data-principal rights) are phased to roughly 13 May 2027 and are not in force yet; erasure is purpose-anchored and yields to retention "necessary for compliance with law"; s.17(1)(a) exempts processing necessary for "enforcing any legal right or claim" (defensive legal holds unsettled); s.38 preserves sectoral retention laws; a Constitution Bench challenge is pending.
